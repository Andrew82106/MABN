# Low-prevalence pressure diagnostic

This is a prevalence-shift diagnostic over the frozen `independent_mas_v3`
outer-OOF predictions. It does not refit the monitor and is not an
independent test set. The development benchmark has 33.625% positives; the
report reweights the same predictions to 5% and 10% positive prevalence.

| target prevalence | method | AUROC | AUPRC | Brier | pooled 5% FPR recall |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 5% | two-line BN fusion | 0.820 | 0.201 | 0.111 | 0.297 |
| 5% | runtime logistic | 0.805 | 0.185 | 0.115 | 0.288 |
| 10% | two-line BN fusion | 0.820 | 0.336 | 0.120 | 0.297 |
| 10% | runtime logistic | 0.805 | 0.314 | 0.124 | 0.288 |

AUROC and the pooled negative-quantile operating point are prevalence
invariant; precision-recall and calibration are not. This result is therefore
reported as a deployment-pressure check, not as a new held-out cohort or a
claim of independent low-base-rate validation. Full values are in
`metrics.json`, generated by `scripts/evaluate_prevalence_shift_v1.py`.
